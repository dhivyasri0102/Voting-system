# Accessibility & Senior Citizen Voting Standards

## 1. Compliance Mandates
Conforms to Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016 and WCAG 2.1 Level AA Accessibility Standards:

1. **Senior Citizen Mode**:
   - Minimum font size increased to 18px with high-contrast text ratios (exceeding 7:1 against backgrounds).
   - Touch targets enlarged to 48px x 48px to accommodate tremor and reduced fine-motor coordination.
   - Elimination of timed out popups or aggressive countdown bars that induce user distress.
2. **Bilingual Vernacular Support**:
   - Full English and Tamil (தமிழ்) translation dictionary covering every form label, prompt, and legal notice.
3. **Web Speech Audio Assistance**:
   - Integrated synthetic voice instruction module reading directions in the resident's selected language.
4. **Assisted Voting Framework**:
   - Explicit operational protocol: companion or election volunteer may navigate screens, but is constitutionally prohibited from selecting candidates or viewing private ballot commitments.
